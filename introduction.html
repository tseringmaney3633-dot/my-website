Introduction to Nipah Virus

✦ Zoonotic Pathogen | Henipavirus | Paramyxoviridae ✦

Nipah virus (NiV) is a highly pathogenic, zoonotic virus belonging to the genus Henipavirus of the family Paramyxoviridae. It can cause severe disease in humans, including Nipah virus encephalitis and respiratory illness, with a high case-fatality rate.

🦇 Fruit bats of the genus Pteropus are considered the natural reservoir of the virus. Transmission to humans can occur through contact with infected animals, contaminated food, or infected individuals.
