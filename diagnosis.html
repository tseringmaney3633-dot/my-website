🧪 RT-PCR · Serology · Isolation 🛡️ BSL-4 required
⚠️ strict biosafety
🧬 RT‑PCR

Gold standard · acute phase

First-choice for detection of viral RNA in:

  • Throat swabs · blood · urine
  • Cerebrospinal fluid (CSF)

N gene assays: highly sensitive, but occasional false negatives due to sequence mismatch with circulating strains.

⏱️ acute infection
📌 preferred for early diagnosis
🧫 ELISA

Serology · IgM & IgG

  • IgM – early acute infection; peaks ~9 days, lasts ~3 months
  • IgG – long-term antibodies (persist >8 months) for retrospective & surveillance

Detects anti‑NiV antibodies in serum.

🔄 acute + retrospective
📊 epidemiological surveillance
🧪 Virus Isolation

BSL‑4 · Vero‑E6 cells

Performed exclusively in BSL‑4 laboratories using permissive Vero‑E6 cell lines.

  • Rapid cytopathic effects (CPE) within 3 days
  • Large syncytia & punctate holes in monolayer
⚠️ BSL‑4 only
