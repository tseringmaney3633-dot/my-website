01

Clinical supportive care

With no licensed antiviral or vaccine, supportive treatment remains the backbone of case management.

🔄 Fluid & electrolyte balance

Close monitoring and correction to stabilize patients through the acute phase.

⚡ Seizure management

Anticonvulsant treatment for the seizures and myoclonus seen in encephalitic disease.

🫁 Mechanical ventilation

Invasive ventilation for patients progressing to severe respiratory failure or ARDS.

02

Pharmacotherapy — off-label & compassionate use

No agent is licensed for NiV, but several have been used under open-label trials, compassionate-use protocols, or evaluated in pre-clinical challenge models.

Ribavirin

Broad-spectrum guanosine analog evaluated in human outbreaks. Open-label trials in Malaysia and Kerala reported relative mortality reductions, but animal challenge models (Syrian hamsters, African green monkeys) found it largely ineffective at preventing death — clinical efficacy in humans remains debated. Also used as post-exposure prophylaxis for high-risk healthcare exposures.

Remdesivir & Favipiravir

Broad-spectrum antivirals with strong pre-clinical results. Remdesivir gave complete protection in African green monkeys challenged with lethal NiV-B when given 24 hours post-infection. Favipiravir fully protected Syrian hamsters in lethal challenge models.

Monoclonal antibody — m102.4

A fully humanized monoclonal antibody targeting the G attachment glycoprotein, blocking receptor binding. Protected non-human primates when given up to 3 days post-exposure, and has been used under compassionate-use protocols in Australia, India, and the US following high-risk exposures.

03

Prevention & physical barriers

🌴 Date palm sap

Blocking the sap route

Informational campaigns in Bangladesh discourage raw sap consumption.

  • Bamboo skirts ("banas") installed around palm trunks to physically block bat access
  • Boiling raw sap inactivates the virus
  • Fermenting sap into molasses also inactivates it
🏥 Healthcare settings

Infection control

Suspected patients are placed in negative-pressure airborne isolation rooms.

  • Standard, contact, and airborne precautions strictly followed
  • N95 respirators, gowns, gloves, protective eyewear required
  • Rigorous surface disinfection — virus can persist on bedsheets and clinical surfaces
🌍 Systems-level

"One Health" approach

Fruit bat ecology, agricultural setups, and domestic livestock interact in complex ways.

  • Integrated veterinary, medical, and ecological surveillance
  • Collaborative strategy across sectors, not siloed response
