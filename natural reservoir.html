Gentle Hosts

The evolutionary home of Nipah virus lies within fruit bats of the genus Pteropus, spanning Asia, Oceania, and Africa — directly overlapping with every documented outbreak zone.

Key reservoir species

P. giganteus India · Bangladesh P. vampyrus Malaysia · Indonesia P. hypomelanus Malaysia · Indonesia P. lylei Thailand · Cambodia

Other bat species

  • Rousettus amplexicaudatus (cave bat) – East Timor
  • Eidolon helvum (straw-coloured fruit bat) – Ghana
Pteropus distribution directly overlaps with every Nipah outbreak zone.

Co-Evolution

Nipah virus has co-evolved with its bat hosts over centuries, exerting virtually no virulence on its natural reservoir.

  • Subclinical Carrying: Bats remain healthy with no pathological damage.
  • Environmental Dissemination: Shed in saliva, urine, feces, and seminal fluid.
  • Environmental Stability: Half-life of 18 hours in bat urine; survives days in fruit pulp or palm sap.
half-life: 18 h shed in multiple fluids

Antiviral Shields

The biological secret behind bats' tolerance lies in their extraordinary, adapted immune system.

Constitutive IFN-α

Permanently active interferon system — immediate restriction without chronic inflammation.

Tetherin

Blocks viral replication and budding within bat cells.

Delayed Clearance

Low B-cell affinity maturation → low-titer antibodies → viral persistence.

Key insight: permanent IFN shield + tetherin + delayed clearance = viral persistence without disease.
IFN-α: always on tetherin blocks budding low B-cell maturation
